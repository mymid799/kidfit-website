# The Story Blueprint Library (The Templates)

To prevent the AI from generating repetitive or overly complex stories, the backend "Orchestrator" randomly selects a blueprint from this library. This gives the illusion of infinite possibilities while keeping the narrative extremely secure and educational.

## System Configuration Rules
*   **The Energy Rule:** The system alternates. If a child plays twice, it will not give them two high-energy "Draw" blueprints back-to-back. It mixes Draw (high energy) with Choice (low energy).
*   **Parental Override:** Parents can select a weekly "Focus" (e.g., Focus on EQ), which weights the dice roll toward specific pillars.

---

## Pillar 1: STEM, Logic & Problem Solving
*Focus: Engineering, physics, shapes, and spatial reasoning.*

**1. The Action Adventure (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** Crossing an uncrossable gap.
*   **Interaction:** "Làm sao để qua sông?" (How do we cross?). Child draws a bridge, boat, or jetpack.
*   **Reward:** Creative Engineer Sticker.

**2. The Builder (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** Preparing for a gentle weather event (rain/wind).
*   **Interaction:** "Bé hãy vẽ một cái mái nhà thật vững chắc nhé!" (Draw a strong roof/fort!).
*   **Reward:** Master Architect Sticker.

**3. The Speed Race (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** A friendly downhill race.
*   **Interaction:** "Bé vẽ xe trượt đi!" (Draw a sled/vehicle!).
*   **Reward:** Lightning Fast Sticker.

## Pillar 2: Social-Emotional Learning (EQ)
*Focus: Identifying feelings, empathy, sharing.*

**4. The Empathy Test (Choice)**
*   **Concept:** Someone is in need (cold, lost).
*   **Interaction:** "Bạn Thỏ đang lạnh, có nên chia ô không?" (Share the umbrella?). Child chooses to help.
*   **Reward:** Kindness Acorn Sticker.

**5. The Mystery (Choice)**
*   **Concept:** A strange (but non-scary) sound in the woods.
*   **Interaction:** "Nên rón rén tò mò, hay dũng cảm bước vào?" (Curious or brave?). Teaches emotional state words.
*   **Reward:** Brave Explorer Sticker.

**6. The Great Rescue (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** Something cute is stuck high up (a baby star in a tree).
*   **Interaction:** "Làm sao để với tới đỉnh cây?" (How to reach the top?). Child draws a ladder or trampoline.
*   **Reward:** Little Hero Sticker.

## Pillar 3: Arts, Expression & Sensory
*Focus: Colors, music, surrealism.*

**7. The Talent Show (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** A forest festival performance.
*   **Interaction:** "Bé hãy vẽ một nhạc cụ nhé!" (Draw a musical instrument!).
*   **Reward:** Super Star Sticker.

**8. The Magical Transformation (Choice)**
*   **Concept:** The hero needs to change color to hide or fly.
*   **Interaction:** "Pha thuốc màu gì? Đỏ (Khỏe), Xanh (Tàng hình), Vàng (Bay)?" (Pick a potion color!).
*   **Reward:** Magic Alchemist Sticker.

**9. The Dream World (Choice)**
*   **Concept:** The hero falls asleep. Abstract surrealism.
*   **Interaction:** "Sẽ mơ thấy gì? Kẹo Bông Bắn Pháo Hoa hay Cá Voi Bay?" (Cotton candy fireworks or flying whales?).
*   **Reward:** Dream Weaver Sticker.

## Pillar 4: Vocabulary & Spatial Awareness
*Focus: Prepositions, directional words, nouns.*

**10. The Silly Mishap (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** Floating away after eating a magic fruit.
*   **Interaction:** "Làm sao để chạm đất?" (How to get down?). Child draws heavy object or anchor.
*   **Reward:** Physics Master Sticker.

**11. Hide and Seek (Choice)**
*   **Concept:** Looking for a fairy friend.
*   **Interaction:** "Tìm ở đâu? Trong hang, Sau thác nước, hay Dưới lá?" (In cave, behind waterfall, under leaf?). Teaches spatial words.
*   **Reward:** Master Detective Sticker.

**12. The Hungry Chef (Draw)**
*   **Concept:** Making the ultimate magic sandwich.
*   **Interaction:** "Bé vẽ món gì kẹp vào bánh mì?" (Draw an ingredient!).
*   **Reward:** Magic Chef Sticker.
