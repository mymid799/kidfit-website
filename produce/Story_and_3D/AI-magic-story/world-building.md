# The Shared Universe: "Lục Địa Trạng Nguyên" (Trạng Nguyên Continent)

This is the ultimate safety guardrail and community builder. By forcing the AI to generate stories *only* within this predefined World Bible, stories remain highly consistent, safe, and shareable among children.

Instead of expanding infinitely to hundreds of disconnected areas, we focus on **Depth and Richness**. We have 5 Core Biomes and 5 Core Guardians (NPCs). They can mix and match, but they are highly defined.

## I. The 5 Core Biomes (The Map)
Each biome has specific landmarks and visual rules to ensure the AI Image generator produces breathtaking, consistent backgrounds.

### 1. Khu Rừng Thì Thầm (The Whispering Forest)
*   **Vibe:** Nature, earthy colors (greens, browns), dappled sunlight.
*   **Landmarks:** "Cây Tri Thức" (The giant Tree of Knowledge with glowing leaves), "Suối Gợi Ý" (The Hint Brook).
*   **Educational Focus:** Biology, empathy, hiding, seeking.
*   **AI Visual Prompt Guardrail:** `[Pixar-style lush forest, giant glowing mushrooms, volumetric sunlight rays, safe and friendly atmosphere, no dark shadows]`.

### 2. Thung Lũng Kẹo Ngọt (Sweet Candy Valley)
*   **Vibe:** Bright, surreal, funny, pastel colors (pinks, yellows).
*   **Landmarks:** "Dòng Sông Sữa" (The Milk River), "Núi Bánh Quy" (Biscuit Mountain).
*   **Educational Focus:** Senses, comedy, colors, shapes.
*   **AI Visual Prompt Guardrail:** `[Surreal Pixar landscape made of sweets, cotton candy clouds, chocolate rivers, bright pastel lighting, very cute and soft]`.

### 3. Thành Phố Mây Khổng Lồ (Giant Cloud City)
*   **Vibe:** Air, architecture, soft whites and sky blues. 
*   **Landmarks:** "Cầu Vồng Dệt" (Bridges woven from solid rainbows), "Đài Quan Sát Gió" (The Wind Observatory).
*   **Educational Focus:** Building, weather, physics (dropping, flying, wind).
*   **AI Visual Prompt Guardrail:** `[Soft fluffy cloud architecture, rainbow bridges, clear blue sky, floating islands, smooth Pixar aesthetic]`.

### 4. Trạm Vũ Trụ Ánh Sao (Starlight Space Station)
*   **Vibe:** Technology, stars, dark blue and friendly neon colors.
*   **Landmarks:** "Phòng Thí Nghiệm Vui Sự" (The Joyful Lab), "Vườn Sao" (The Star Garden where baby stars grow).
*   **Educational Focus:** Engineering, logic, vehicles, problem-solving.
*   **AI Visual Prompt Guardrail:** `[Friendly sci-fi space station interior, colorful glowing buttons, big windows showing starry outer space, toy-like machinery, cute Pixar style]`.

### 5. Đại Dương Pha Lê (Crystal Ocean)
*   **Vibe:** Underwater (but the characters can breathe freely), bioluminescent light, fluid movement.
*   **Landmarks:** "Rạn San Hô Phát Sáng" (Glowing Coral Reef), "Nhạc Hội Dưới Đáy Biển" (The Deep Sea Concert Hall).
*   **Educational Focus:** Music, rhythm, observation, patience.
*   **AI Visual Prompt Guardrail:** `[Colorful underwater Pixar world, glowing crystal coral reefs, clear blue water, shafts of light from above, friendly sea life]`.

## II. The 5 Guardians (Core NPCs)
To ensure the AI doesn't hallucinate random terrifying characters, we hardcode 5 NPCs. The child's drawing is the Hero, but they always meet one of these Guardians. Having a strict visual prompt for each ensures they look the *exact same* in every child's app.

### 1. Bác Cú Thông Thái (Professor Owl)
*   **Home:** Whispering Forest
*   **Personality:** Wise, asks a lot of questions, sometimes loses his glasses.
*   **Role:** The Logic guide. Appears in Mystery and Builder templates.
*   **AI Visual Prompt:** `[Cute Pixar owl wearing oversized round glasses, holding a glowing green book, fluffy feathers]`.

### 2. Thỏ Út Ít (Tiny Rabbit)
*   **Home:** Sweet Candy Valley
*   **Personality:** Sweet, clumsy, easily scared but very thankful. Always carries a magical oversized carrot.
*   **Role:** The Empathy test. Appears in Rescue and Sharing templates.
*   **AI Visual Prompt:** `[Tiny fluffy white Pixar rabbit, incredibly expressive cute eyes, holding a comically large orange carrot]`.

### 3. Rô-bốt Lạch Cạch (Clanky the Robot)
*   **Home:** Starlight Space Station
*   **Personality:** Enthusiastic but his inventions always break down. Speaks in funny beeps.
*   **Role:** The STEM guide. Appears in Action, Racing, and Problem-Solving templates.
*   **AI Visual Prompt:** `[Friendly square-shaped robot made of colorful plastic toy blocks, glowing blue face screen showing a smiley face, Pixar style]`.

### 4. Cáo Nghệ Sĩ (Fanny the Fox)
*   **Home:** Giant Cloud City
*   **Personality:** Dramatic, highly emotional, loves painting and singing.
*   **Role:** The Arts guide. Appears in Talent Show, Color Transformation, and Emotion templates.
*   **AI Visual Prompt:** `[Adorable orange Pixar fox wearing a beret, paint smudges on cheeks, holding a magical glowing paintbrush]`.

### 5. Rùa Lạc Quan (Happy Turtle)
*   **Home:** Crystal Ocean
*   **Personality:** Very slow, extremely chill, always has the answer if you wait long enough. 
*   **Role:** The Patience guide. Appears in Hide & Seek and Spatial Logic templates.
*   **AI Visual Prompt:** `[Round cute Pixar sea turtle, wearing a tiny red neckerchief, shell shines like polished opal]`.

## III. Why "Enriching" beats "Expanding"

Adding 50 biomes and 200 characters is a mistake for Generative AI. 
1. **AI Prompts Decay:** The more characters the AI has to remember, the worse the images get. By locking in exactly 5 Biome Prompts and 5 Character Prompts, the images generated will be **stunning, perfectly consistent, and high-fidelity every single time**.
2. **The "Pokemon" Effect:** Children attach to characters deeply. By seeing Professor Owl or Clanky the Robot repeatedly across different story templates, they form a bond with them.
3. **The Playground Talk:** This is the ultimate goal. 
   - Child A: *"My drawn cat helped Clanky fix his rocket in the Cloud City!"*
   - Child B: *"Wow! My drawn car raced Clanky in the Whispering Forest!"*
   They are sharing a universe.
