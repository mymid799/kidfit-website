# Background Music — CC0 / Royalty-Free

Place your copyright-free music files here for the AI Storyboard Player.

## Expected file names:
- `gentle-lullaby.mp3` — For calm, peaceful stories
- `happy-adventure.mp3` — For exciting, joyful stories  
- `magical-wonder.mp3` — For magical, curious stories

## Where to download CC0 music (Free for commercial use):
- **Pixabay Music**: https://pixabay.com/music/ (No attribution required)
- **Free Music Archive**: https://freemusicarchive.org/ (Filter by CC0)
- **Incompetech**: https://incompetech.com/ (Attribution required, but free)

## Tips:
- Keep files under 2MB each (30-60 second loops work best)
- MP3 format, 128kbps is sufficient for background music
- Choose gentle, child-friendly instrumental tracks
- The player will automatically loop the music
